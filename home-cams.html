Home Cams

A private home camera system, run by Mark McKellar for his own household.

Home Cams records motion from a few home cameras, uses an AI model running on our own computer to say whether each clip shows a person, cat, dog or raccoon, and lets the household browse the clips in a web app.

Who can use it

Only people Mark approves. Anyone can sign in with a Google account, but a new account sees nothing until it is approved and given access to a household's cameras.

How it's built

The code is open source: github.com/markmckellar/cmo.
