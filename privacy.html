Home Cams privacy policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Home Cams is a private camera system run by Mark McKellar for his household. This page explains what it does with your information.

Signing in with Google

When you sign in with Google, Home Cams receives your name, email address and Google account ID. It uses them only to decide whether you may see a household's cameras, to tell Mark that you asked for access, and to keep you signed in. It does not get your Google password, contacts, files or anything else from your account.

What is stored

  • Your email address and name, on the Home Cams server, as a pending request or an approved member.
  • A sign-in cookie in your browser, so you stay signed in.
  • Camera clips recorded by the household's own cameras, and the AI's label for each, kept for up to 180 days.

Sharing

Your information is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything else. The camera clips are analyzed by an AI model on our own computer, not sent to an outside AI service.

Removing your information

Email mckellar.mm@gmail.com to have your access and your stored name and email removed.
